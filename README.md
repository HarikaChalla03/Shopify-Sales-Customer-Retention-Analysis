# Shopify-Sales-Customer-Retention-Analysis
An end-to-end Power BI project that analyzes Shopify sales performance, customer retention, repeat purchase behavior, and customer lifetime value (CLV) to identify revenue growth opportunities.

# Business Problem 
Shopify businesses often have access to sales data but lack visibility into customer retention, repeat purchase behavior, and customer lifetime value. Without these insights, it becomes difficult to identify loyal customers, optimize marketing strategies, and drive sustainable revenue growth.

# Business Objectives
Analyze overall sales performance and revenue trends.
Measure customer retention and repeat purchase rates.
Identify high-value customers using Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).
Evaluate product and geographic performance.
Generate actionable recommendations to improve customer retention and revenue.
